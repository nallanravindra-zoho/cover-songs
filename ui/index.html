2Lyrics

Type or paste the lyrics in Telugu or Hindi letters. A line like [pallavi] starts each part. The last part, [order], says in which order the parts are sung. For humming or an aalap with no words, add a block called [Aalap] (with what you want shown, for example ఓ...ఓ...), and write Aalap in the order where it is sung, e.g. Aalap, pallavi, charanam 1, pallavi, Aalap. The tool finds where it is sung from the voice. A long line gets a screen of its own, split into two lines.

3Files

Add the lyrics first (step 2), then the video here. Drop each file on its box, or press Choose. Big files take a minute; the bar shows progress. As soon as the lyrics and the video are both in, the lyrics are placed on the voice automatically (the progress panel at the top shows what is happening).

How the raw video lines up with the mastered audio

4Interludes & transitions

The lyrics are placed on her voice automatically. Change any time on the timeline in Preview & edit (drag the blocks). Here: which instrumental gaps get pictures, and the transitions.

Interludes and their transitions

Found automatically from the gaps in the singing (change the gap length in step 5). The list updates as you change the lyric times, the gap, or the styles. Pick a transition for any interlude, or leave it on automatic.

Transition styles

Click a place in the video, then click one style below (one style per place). Each tile plays a short sample using two pictures from her video.

5Photos

Every instrumental gap longer than the number below gets a picture. If you add no photos, a plain default picture is used. Add photos, then pick the picture for each interlude here. Which gaps get a picture, and their transitions, are in step 4.

Snapshots from your video

Sharp still frames of the video as PNG pictures. Use any of them as an interlude photo, or download them to edit elsewhere. They are also saved in the song's snapshots folder.
